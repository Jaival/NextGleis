NextGleis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

NextGleis is a departure-board app for German bus and train stops. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

No accounts, no sign-up

NextGleis does not require or support account creation. There is no login, and no personal identity information (name, email, phone number, etc.) is collected by the app.

What's stored on your device

Your favorite stations and routes, and any per-station line filters you set, are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party. It stays on your device until you remove a favorite, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

What's sent over the network

When you search for a stop, open a departure board, or look up a route, your device sends that request (the stop name you typed, or the IDs of the stops involved) to NextGleis's own backend server. The backend relays it to the public journey planners of German transport networks (and, for stations saved in older versions of the app, possibly to Deutsche Bahn's Timetables API) to fetch schedule and delay information, and returns the result to the app. No account identifiers, device identifiers, or personal data are attached to these requests beyond what's technically necessary to make the network request (e.g. your IP address, as with any internet request).

NextGleis's backend does not log or store your search queries or requests beyond a short cache (at most a few minutes for searches, boards, and routes, and up to a day for the position of a stop) used to avoid overloading the upstream data sources.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

NextGleis does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers of any kind. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Third-party data sources

Departure, route, and delay data comes from the HAFAS journey planners of German transport networks and associations (RMV, NVV, VBB, VBN, VOS, VSN, NAH.SH, INSA, VMT, RSAG, AVV, Saarfahrplan, S-Bahn München, and INVG), accessed with the open-source hafas-client library.

For stations saved in older versions of the app, data may instead be provided by Deutsche Bahn AG / DB InfraGO AG via the DB API Marketplace "Timetables" product, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Use of that data is subject to Deutsche Bahn's terms of use.

Children's privacy

NextGleis does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, since no personal data is collected from any user in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If NextGleis's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.

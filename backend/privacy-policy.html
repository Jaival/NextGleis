NextGleis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

NextGleis is a departure-board app for German bus and train stops. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

No accounts, no sign-up

NextGleis does not require or support account creation. There is no login, and no personal identity information (name, email, phone number, etc.) is collected by the app.

What's stored on your device

Your favorite stations and any per-station line filters you set are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party. It stays on your device until you remove a favorite, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

What's sent over the network

When you search for a station or open a departure board, your device sends that request (the station name you typed, or the station's ID) to NextGleis's own backend server, which relays it to Deutsche Bahn's public Timetables API to fetch schedule and delay information, and returns the result to the app. No account identifiers, device identifiers, or personal data are attached to these requests beyond what's technically necessary to make the network request (e.g. your IP address, as with any internet request).

NextGleis's backend does not log or store your search queries or station requests beyond a short (under one minute) cache used to avoid overloading the upstream data source.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

NextGleis does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers of any kind. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Third-party data source

Departure and delay data is provided by Deutsche Bahn AG / DB InfraGO AG via the DB API Marketplace "Timetables" product, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Use of that data is subject to Deutsche Bahn's terms of use.

Children's privacy

NextGleis does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, since no personal data is collected from any user in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If NextGleis's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
